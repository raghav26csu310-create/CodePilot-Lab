About

Built to make the machine visible.

CodePilot Lab is a local-first coding laboratory. It exists because most “AI coding” surfaces hide two things at once: the program that actually ran, and the evidence behind the model's answer.

Principles

  • No invented data. Projects, timestamps, execution output, trace events, metrics and health all come from real browser, IndexedDB, runtime or WebGL APIs.
  • No silent writes. AI proposals are reviewed as diffs; saves stay manual; replacements require confirmation.
  • No hidden network. The app runs no backend of its own. The only calls are the provider you configure, the optional feedback endpoint and the loopback runner.
  • No pretended safety. The execution service is a resource-limited local runner, not a sandbox, and the product says so in the UI and documentation.
  • No fake social proof. This site publishes no user counts, ratings or testimonials. The beta is real; the audience numbers are simply unknown.

What is honest to call “done”

The workspace, copilot, execution lab, Python tracer, practice arena, converter, settings and feedback are live. Project Atlas, Project Vault and Activity & Diagnostics remain visible placeholders because their real implementations do not exist yet. Scheduled work is labelled on the route itself.

Who builds it

CodePilot Lab is an independent project. There is no company, funding claim or implied team page here. The repository, version and licence terms are the authoritative record.
